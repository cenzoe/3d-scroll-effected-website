The four skills

One test, four separate disciplines.

Every skill is scored on its own, then averaged into your overall band. Strength in one won't cover a gap in another — each needs its own practice.

01

Listening

40 questions · 30 min + transfer time

What it tests

Four recordings, one hearing only. You'll follow a conversation, a monologue, and an academic discussion, then answer as the audio moves on.

02

Reading

40 questions · 60 minutes

What it tests

Three long passages, no extra time to transfer answers. Skimming for structure first is what separates a band 6 from a band 8 here.

Tap for detail
03

Writing

2 tasks · 60 minutes

What it tests

Task 1 describes data or a process, Task 2 argues a position. Task 2 counts double, so time protects it, not the other way round.

Tap for detail
04

Speaking

3 parts · 11–14 minutes

What it tests

A face-to-face conversation, a two-minute solo topic, then a discussion that digs into your opinions. Fluency is judged, not accent.

Band 0

Did not attempt

No assessable evidence was submitted. This is a starting line, not a skill level — everyone above it once stood here too.

Band 3

Extremely limited user

Communication breaks down often, even in familiar situations. At this stage the priority is core vocabulary and basic sentence structure, not exam technique.

Band 4

Limited user

Basic ideas get across in familiar contexts, but complex language causes frequent problems. Reading and listening for gist becomes the next milestone.

Band 5

Modest user

A working command of English that copes with overall meaning in most situations, though errors and misunderstandings are still frequent.

Band 6

Competent user

Generally effective, with occasional inaccuracies and misunderstandings, especially in unfamiliar situations. Most university entry thresholds start here.

Band 7

Good user

Operational command with occasional inaccuracies. Handles complex language well and understands detailed reasoning, with some slips under pressure.

Band 8

Very good user

Fully operational command, with only occasional unsystematic errors. Handles unfamiliar situations well and argues with real nuance.

Band 9

Expert user

Full operational command of English. Appropriate, accurate, and fluent, with complete understanding — the ceiling most candidates never need to reach.
